About Noshahi VirtuLearn

Noshahi VirtuLearn is an innovative e-learning platform designed to enhance online education. It provides structured courses, real-time interaction, and an intuitive learning management system for students, teachers, and administrators.

Key Features

  • Easy course enrollment for students and teachers.
  • Interactive learning materials, including videos, quizzes, and lecture notes.
  • Role-based access control for students, teachers, and principals.
  • Seamless navigation through courses, chapters, and topics.
  • Integrated with Directus for efficient database management.

How It Works

Users can sign up and select their role (Student, Teacher, or Principal). Based on the role, they gain access to specific features:

  • Students: Browse and enroll in courses, track progress, and access learning materials.
  • Teachers: Create, manage, and assign courses, chapters, and quizzes.
  • Principals: Oversee school-wide learning activities, manage users, and generate reports.

With a user-friendly interface and powerful backend, Noshahi VirtuLearn makes online education simple and effective.
